# Collision callbacks

The wrapper supports synchronous collision/overlap queries and automatic contact
and trigger events. See [README.md](README.md#collision-and-trigger-events) for
current examples, delivery timing, listener ownership, and teardown rules.

`CollisionCallback`, `ContactCallbackData`, and `SendPortEventListener` are exported
from `package:reactphysics3d_dart/reactphysics3d_dart.dart`.
